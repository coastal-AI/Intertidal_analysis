\begin{pblock}{Is this site mappable at all?}
DEM quality is bounded above by how well the orbit happened to sample the
tidal frame — a site whose acquisitions all fall near mid-tide cannot yield a
good model however the fitting is done. We therefore compute the sampling
diagnostics \emph{before} the study and report them \emph{with} the results:
fraction of the tidal range covered, uniformity against a uniform
distribution, entropy, largest gap, and the resulting vertical sampling
resolution.

This turns a silent failure mode into a stated precondition, and it is what
tells us in advance that Vadehavet is the hard site: a modelled tidal range of
1.90\,m against a flat spanning 2.19\,m, so the extremes of
the surface are never observed at all.
\end{pblock}
