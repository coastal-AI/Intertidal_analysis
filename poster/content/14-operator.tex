\begin{pblock}{What is measurable — and the operator that respects it}
The binary likelihood is exactly invariant under
$(\alpha, z, \sigma)\!\to\!(c\alpha, cz, c\sigma)$: a theorem, not a limit
of effort. It dictates the division of labour:

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l l@{}}
\toprule
Quantity & From imagery? & Anchor \\
\midrule
Phase lag $\tau(s)$      & \term{Yes} — $\pm$5\,min & mouth band, $\tau=0$ \\
Boundary phase error     & \term{Yes} — $\pm$4\,min & mouth pixels audit it \\
Amplitude / gain         & No (theorem)             & ocean-model ensemble \\
Vertical datum           & No (theorem)             & ocean model \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{3mm}
A planted $+12$\,min error of the boundary model masquerades as
\emphnum{up to $+21$\,min} of fictitious ``estuarine transfer'' — the mouth
audit removes it before it can. The final operator
$h(s,t)=h_B\!\left(t-\tau(s)\right)$ carries a lag \emph{only} where it
beats a matched null (the archive re-simulated with a uniform tide: same
pixels, same clouds, same noise), and passed its own gate: planted damage
recovered, no harm at the mouth, and a fixed point of its own estimation.
Never calibrated with tide gauges — gauges only \emph{validate} where they
exist.
