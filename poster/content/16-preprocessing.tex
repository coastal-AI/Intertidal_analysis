\begin{pblock}{Before any tide: masks, clouds, detector}
\defn{The intertidal mask, from the data}{Every pixel votes across the
archive: permanent water, permanent land, or \term{transition}. The
transition class, filtered by its water frequency against the tidal
window, is the intertidal — \emphnum{31{,}315} pixels in Villaviciosa,
no chart or DEM consulted.}
\defn{Clouds measured on the flat only}{A cloud three kilometres inland
does not obscure the flat, so cloudiness is scored \emph{inside the
dilated transition zone alone}, per pixel. Only 141 of 1379 scenes are
fully clear — but \emphnum{415} have half the flat visible, and every
clear pixel in them enters the fit.}
\defn{The water detector, chosen by experiment}{Four indices ran over
byte-identical dates and masks, ranked by correlation of water frequency
with LiDAR elevation (a lower pixel must flood more often). \term{NDWI}
wins ($\rho=-0.624$): both bands are native 10\,m, so no resampling
smears the waterline that carries the signal.}
\end{pblock}
