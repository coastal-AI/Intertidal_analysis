\begin{pblock}{Three demonstrated limits (negative results we keep)}
Every claim faced a pre-registered gate against a matched null; three
died with a clean control, and each is a citable boundary for the field:

\defn{Gain is invisible to binary data}{Twice (interior gain, boundary
gain): the likelihood cannot separate amplification from a rescaled
seabed. The pipeline \emph{exposes} the flat likelihood instead of
printing a number.}
\defn{Per-limb clocks are not adoptable at 465 scenes}{Three versions,
three failure modes caught (likelihood runaway, multiple-comparison
adoption, insufficient power under the null threshold). Ponding stays a
simulator mechanism and a quality flag — not a correction. Reopens with
the 10-year archive.}
\defn{Censoring hides itself}{A fully censored pixel refits \emph{at} its
spill level, erasing the geometric evidence of its own pit: any detector
built from the refit map is recall-bounded ($\approx$0.4--0.5, measured).}

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{pcallout}
Point-vs-pixel: comparing GNSS points to 10\,m pixel medians adds
$\sigma\!\approx\!0.21$\,m that belongs to the \emph{yardstick} — the
``slope compression'' this literature reports is largely that artefact
(true $c=1.04$). The method's own error is $\approx$0.13--0.15\,m.
\end{pcallout}
\end{pblock}
