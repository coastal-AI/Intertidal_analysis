\begin{pblock}{The north coast: 130 cells, one census}
Nothing is site-specific: every cell runs the identical pipeline — same
configs, same seeds, zero per-site tuning. Terrain-following cells from the
real coastline, cubes queued server-side on openEO, harvested and processed
in a resumable worker pool; one tide-model call per cell.
