\begin{pblock}{The north coast: 116 cells, one census \wip}
Nothing is site-specific: every cell runs the identical pipeline — same
configs, same seeds, zero per-site tuning. Cubes are queued server-side on
openEO (30 concurrent jobs), harvested and processed in a resumable worker
pool; one tide-model call per cell.
