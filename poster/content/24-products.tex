\defn{$\sigma_z$ per pixel, calibrated}{Tabulated from planted recoveries
in the calibrated digital twin, by tidal headroom and observation count.
Verified conservative: the 68\,\% interval covers \emphnum{83\,\%} of real
errors — never optimistic.}
\defn{$\sigma_{\text{topo}}$ vs.\ level decomposition}{The fitted
transition width carries the tide-model error convolved in; deconvolved,
median sub-pixel relief is \emphnum{0.177\,m} (35\,\% of the width's
variance was water level, not ground).}
\defn{Declared hydraulics}{Depression filling yields each pixel's spill
elevation and ponding depth: \emphnum{14\,\%} of the intertidal sits
behind a barrier (median 1.2\,m). The flag is validated against the real
ponding signature, its bounded recall printed on the product.}
